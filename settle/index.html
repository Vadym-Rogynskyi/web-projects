    Method

    How Settle works

    A transparent model you can check line by line — no black box, no lead forms.

    1

    Size the home

    About one bedroom per two people. Rent and price for each city scale with the bedroom count using standard premiums.

    2

    Price both paths

    Owning includes mortgage, property tax, insurance, upkeep, PMI and HOA where relevant. Renting includes rent and renters insurance.

    3

    Weigh it for you

    The verdict blends the price-to-rent ratio, your time in the home, your down payment and how much of your income each option takes.

    What goes into the monthly cost of buying?

    A 30-year fixed mortgage at your rate and down payment, each state's typical effective property-tax rate, about 0.35% a year for insurance, about 1% a year for maintenance, mortgage insurance (PMI) when you put down less than 20%, and a $280/month HOA estimate in condo- and co-op-heavy markets.

    How is the break-even year calculated?

    For every month, Settle tracks what each path really costs. Buying: cash paid in (down payment, ~3% closing costs, monthly owner costs) minus what you'd walk away with if you sold (home value after ~6% selling costs, minus the remaining loan). Renting: rent paid minus what the renter's spare cash (the down payment, closing costs, and any monthly savings) would have earned invested. Break-even is the first year buying's net cost drops below renting's.

    How is the verdict decided?

    A price-to-rent ratio under 15 leans toward buying and over 25 leans toward renting. Staying under 3 years counts against buying, 8+ years counts in favor. If owning would take more than ~43% of gross income it counts strongly against buying, and over 50% renting wins outright. A 20%+ down payment helps; under 10% hurts.

    Where does the data come from — is it live?

    No. Prices and rents are simplified city medians for this educational tool, not live listings. Use them to understand the trade-off, then check current local listings and talk to a lender or agent before deciding.

    Do you store my information?

    No. Everything runs in your browser. Your inputs are kept only in the page link (so sharing works) and in your own browser's storage so the page remembers you. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    These are ballpark estimates. Settle is an educational tool using simplified, non-live city data. It is not financial, tax or legal advice — verify with local sources and a licensed professional before making a decision.
